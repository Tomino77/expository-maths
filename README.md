# expository-maths
Collection of notes about mathematics that interest me. Typical topics I like researching about are group theory, topology, geometry, and knot theory.
Intended to be scripts to be turned into YouTube videos.

**Rubik's Cube Essay (The Rubik's Cube Group)** - A script inspired by a conversation I had about why the Rubik's cube and mathematics are so synonymous with each other, despite solving a cube requiring no mathematical knowledge. It also got me interested in where 'parity' comes from, a term used in both subjects with a not-so obvious crossover. I then look at how we can use the mathematical properties of the Rubik's cube to create a generalised solution to the puzzle.

**Topology Project (Topology from the Group Up)** - A script, also inspired by many conversations, explaining where topology comes from as a research area of mathematics, starting from basic mathematical knowledge, motivating definitions of continuity, and eventually defining homeomorphisms. In future I'd like to add to this project, explaining the fundemental group (supplemented by group theory explanation in the Rubik's Cube Group script), and eventually defining homology as a richer generalisation of the idea behind the fundemental group.
